FAQ

Does this upload my notes to the cloud?

No. Everything runs on your Mac. See SECURITY.md in the repository.

Can I export Apple Notes to Markdown for Obsidian?

Yes. Use --format md (or all). Files include YAML frontmatter and relative links to extracted images.

Do I need Full Disk Access?

Not for core text/HTML export. It can improve attachment recovery from the Notes media store.

What about locked notes?

Locked notes that Automation cannot read are skipped and listed in skipped.json.

Is iCloud supported?

Yes for notes already synced into Notes.app on the Mac. The tool does not call iCloud APIs.
